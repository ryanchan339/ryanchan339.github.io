Overview

Built a local-first search engine that indexes video frames and speech, then returns playable timestamps for natural-language queries.

Search pipeline

SigLIP embeds sampled frames; Whisper transcribes speech for a separate text index. Queries search both indexes with cosine similarity. Standardized scores merge the results, and nearby duplicates are removed.

Engineering decisions

  • Scene sampling. Reduced indexed frames by 42% on benchmark footage while retaining top-1 matches for the validated rabbit, butterfly, and apple queries.
  • Background indexing. Indexed videos outside the search flow, supporting library-wide and per-video search.
  • Ranking control. Let users adjust the balance between spoken and visual matches.

Benchmarks

SigLIP so400m is the precision default; base-224 is the faster mode. On Apple Silicon, indexing measured 2.3 frames/sec for so400m and 42 for base-224. Throughput depends on hardware.

The 42% frame reduction was measured on benchmark footage with a small set of validated queries. It does not establish equal retrieval quality across other videos.
