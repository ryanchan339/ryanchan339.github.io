Stock Trader

A stock-ranking research pipeline with feature engineering, model evaluation, portfolio constraints, and a dashboard that makes every stage inspectable.

RoleIndependent project
Project periodApril–May 2026
Built withPython · scikit-learn · Alpaca

The problem

Make the entire research loop visible

A stock prediction alone does not explain how a portfolio is built or whether a model adds useful information. This project connects data collection, ranking, allocation, and evaluation so the whole decision process can be inspected.

The approach

From prices to a constrained portfolio

The pipeline turns daily market history into momentum, volatility, moving-average, RSI, and cross-sectional features. A gradient-boosted classifier estimates relative performance against SPY. Its ranking is blended with momentum before the top five stocks become portfolio targets.

01LearnBuild features and train on historical observations.
02AllocateRank candidates and apply exposure limits.
03AuditGenerate an order plan and record the outcome.

Engineering

Automation with a reviewable boundary

A scheduled workflow refreshes data, trains the model, and generates recommendations and a dry-run order plan. Individual holdings are capped at 15% and total exposure at 75%. Paper-order submission is an explicit step; the dashboard reads committed report files.

Evaluation

Compare the model with simple alternatives

The dashboard includes backtests, momentum and low-volatility baselines, and five walk-forward folds. The committed walk-forward summary has a mean ROC AUC of about 0.506 and average excess return of roughly −0.7%. That is useful evidence about the model’s limits, even when the headline backtest looks stronger.

Values from the committed dashboard data, generated September 11, 2026. Walk-forward and baseline reports can cover earlier periods than the headline backtest.
