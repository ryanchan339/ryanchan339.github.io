Personal project · April–May 2026

ML Stock Trader

A stock-ranking pipeline with feature engineering, walk-forward evaluation, and paper-trading automation.

PythonPandasNumPyscikit-learnAlpaca API
View source ↗All projects ↗

Overview

Built a pipeline to prepare market data, train and evaluate models, rank equities, and generate constrained portfolios for paper trading.

50+US large-cap equities analyzed
5Walk-forward evaluation folds
15% / 75%Position cap / total exposure cap

Pipeline

Daily prices become momentum, volatility, moving-average, RSI, and cross-sectional features. A gradient-boosted classifier estimates performance relative to SPY. Model scores and momentum determine the top five portfolio targets.

Market data + features
→
Model + ranking
→
Portfolio targets + order plan

Implementation

  • Features. Python, Pandas, and NumPy prepare training and ranking data.
  • Allocation. Select five stocks, rebalance every five trading days, and cap each holding at 15% and total exposure at 75%.
  • Automation. A scheduled workflow refreshes data, trains the model, and generates a dry-run order plan. Submitting paper orders through Alpaca is a separate step.
  • Dashboard. Display predictions, comparisons, and recorded activity from committed reports.

Evaluation

Compared backtests with momentum and low-volatility baselines and evaluated five walk-forward folds. The committed summary reports a mean ROC AUC of about 0.506 and average excess return of roughly −0.7%, showing limited predictive value.

Dashboard data was generated September 11, 2026. Walk-forward and baseline reports can cover different periods from the headline backtest; returns are not directly comparable. Backtests, simulations, and paper-account results are distinct.
