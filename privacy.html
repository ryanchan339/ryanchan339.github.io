Privacy

How this site uses visitor analytics.

What this site measures

This portfolio uses Umami Cloud when analytics is enabled. It measures page visits, referring websites, approximate country, browser language and screen size, and clicks on links such as the resume, project writeups, GitHub, and email.

This integration does not use analytics cookies or send your name, email address, or anything you type. It does not use session replay or identify individual visitors by name or employer. No special application-tracking links are used.

How it works

Your browser contacts Umami Cloud directly. Umami processes technical request information, including IP addresses, to produce statistics and approximate locations. Its infrastructure may retain separate operational or security logs. The site sends the page path without query parameters and limits referral information to the referring website’s origin. Link events use fixed labels, never form contents.

Learn more in Umami Cloud’s privacy information ↗.

Your choice

Analytics runs only when your browser preferences allow it.

This preference is stored only in this browser. The site also respects Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Ad blockers may block analytics independently.
